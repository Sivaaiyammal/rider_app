At VM Routes, we prioritize your data privacy and security. Here's what you need to know:

  • Limited Data Collection: We collect only necessary information for app functionality – your name, contact details, vehicle info, and location for trip purposes.
  • Strict Security Measures: Your data is protected with strong security protocols, ensuring it remains confidential and inaccessible to unauthorized parties.
  • No Third-Party Sharing: We don’t share or sell your data to third parties for commercial purposes. Information is used solely within the app for trip assignments and navigation.
  • Transparency and Control: You have control over your data within the app and can update it as needed. Our privacy policies are clear and easily accessible.
  • Compliance and Retention: We adhere to data protection laws and retain your data only as long as necessary for app services.

Your trust matters. VM Routes is committed to keeping your data safe and using it responsibly for app functionality.
