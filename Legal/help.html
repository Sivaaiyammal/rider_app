Here's how to get started:

  • Account Setup: Sign up by providing accurate information and necessary documents. Once approved, you'll be ready to start accepting trips.
  • Accepting Trips: When a trip request comes in, review the details, and accept it if you're available. Navigate to the pickup location using the in-app navigation.
  • Safety First: Ensure passenger safety by following traffic laws and maintaining a professional and courteous demeanor.
  • App Updates: Keep the app updated to access new features and improvements for a smoother experience.
  • Support and Assistance: If you have any questions or encounter issues, reach out to our support team via the app's help section.

Remember, your safety and professionalism are crucial. Enjoy driving with VM Routes!
